# Fly Brain LeetCode

> All 138,639 neurons of a real fruit fly, frozen exactly as mapped by FlyWire, solving LeetCode.

## Results

## Story

This started as a follow up to an interview practice tool I had built. The fly's synapses never change, and only a small readout on its descending neurons is trained, so whatever it solves is the fly's own dynamics doing the work.
