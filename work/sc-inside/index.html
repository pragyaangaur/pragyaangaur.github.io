Inside the chimney

Flue gas rises through a copper catalyst bed and leaves as clean air. The sulphur comes out the side as acid.

Most sulphur cleanup means building a new scrubber tower next to the plant. SkyCatcher drops into the chimney or duct that is already there, so the factory keeps its skyline.
