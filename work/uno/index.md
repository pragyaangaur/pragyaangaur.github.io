# How long is a game of Uno?

> I couldn't find out, so I proved it. With N players holding M cards each, a round lasts (27/19)NM turns.

October 2026. Section: Maths.

Paper: [How long is a game of Uno? A counting law for large hands and the Wild Draw Four hoard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14076)

## Results

- 58 million games simulated in C, and big hands quietly hoard M/23 Wild Draw Fours each
- Chess grandmaster Karsten Müller read it and wrote to me about its Nash equilibrium

## Story

On 7 October 2026 I wondered whether anyone knew how long a game of Uno lasts, and the answer was mostly no. So I simulated 58 million games, and a day later the paper was on Zenodo.
