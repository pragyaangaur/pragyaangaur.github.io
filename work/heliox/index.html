Heliox

I rebuilt the core of SunPy from scratch, then moved it into Rust. Where the Sun runs slow, Rust takes over.

  • Kernels for other sunpy packages, up to 390x faster and bit-identical to the original

November 2026Heliox began as SunPy rebuilt offline to see how a solar toolkit fits together. In November 2026 its core moved into a Rust crate, with every ported function checked against the Python it replaced. A separate package then took on slow functions from other sunpy packages, and the sunkit-image one runs about 390 times faster on a real velocity field.
