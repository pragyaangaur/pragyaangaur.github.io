The Sun, drawn with equations

Every pixel of a 2000 by 2000 Sun comes from one page of formulas. After Hamid Naderi Yeganeh.

I drew the Sun the way Hamid Naderi Yeganeh draws his pictures, with a closed formula for every pixel. There is no random number generator and no image filter, and the whole picture is secretly a temperature map.
