# SkyCatcher

> Factory chimneys in India pour out sulphur dioxide. SkyCatcher sits inside the chimney and turns it into sulphuric acid that can be sold, on a copper catalyst that keeps renewing itself.

Founder, since July 2024. September 2026. Section: The reactor.

## Results

- Patent pending in India, complete specification filed September 2026
- TRL 4 validated, and backed by $30,000+ in grants
- Pitched twice at the beVisioneers Global Summit in Stuttgart

## Story

SkyCatcher started with a simple wish to stop chimneys in India from breathing sulphur dioxide on everyone. The complete patent specification went to the Indian Patent Office on 17 September 2026, with every form and fee filed the same day.
