# Residual Tones

> What excitement, sadness and pain inside a model sound like, and whether another model can hear them.

October 2026. Section: AI, LLMs, ML and cognitive neuroscience.

## Story

I took the emotion directions from the Pain Axis study and turned them into sound and music. Then the sounds were judged blind, to see whether a listener could tell which feeling was which.
