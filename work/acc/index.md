# A measured neighbourhood of the Andrews-Curtis conjecture

> The Andrews-Curtis conjecture has been open since 1965. This census maps the ground around it, and every claim comes with a certificate you can replay or a search that ran to the end.

November 2026. Section: Maths.

Paper: [A measured neighbourhood of the Andrews-Curtis conjecture](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24035724)

## Results

- Every presentation up to total length 14 classified. At most 20 AC-classes are left at that length
- Everything reachable from AK(3) up to total length 25 searched, 2.9 billion orbits, and the standard presentation is not there
- 829 Lean 4 files, and two entries in the SAIR and Caltech Andrews-Curtis Challenge

## Story

The SAIR Foundation and Caltech ran a challenge on the conjecture, and I entered both of my Proof Track results. Next to the entry I ran a research campaign of my own, and it ended in a 22 page census paper with fifteen small corrections to published statements.
