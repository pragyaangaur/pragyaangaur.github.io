# Kerf

> Version control that understands CAD geometry. Change a bracket and Kerf shows what moved, merges two branches, and refuses a merge that breaks the part.

August 2026. Section: Random things.

## Story

Git tracks bytes, and a CAD file is bytes that mean something. I built Kerf over 30 and 31 August 2026 in 105 commits, and the playground runs the geometry, the diff and the merge in your browser.
