# Left alone with a pain button

> A preregistered test of whether a language model left alone will press a button that steers it into pain.

October 2026. Section: AI, LLMs, ML and cognitive neuroscience.

Paper: [Left Alone with a Pain Button](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23510640)

## Story

This is my version of Wilson's famous study where people left alone with their thoughts chose to shock themselves. An idle Qwen 2.5 7B gets a button that brings back a steered pain state, and the question is whether boredom wins.
