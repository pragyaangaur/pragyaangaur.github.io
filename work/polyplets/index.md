# Hyperbolic polyplets

> On the {4,5} tiling of the hyperbolic plane the count of polyplets stopped at seven cells. Two exact programs, built on different maths, both found 1,261,889 at eight.

September 2026. Section: Maths.

## Results

- Approved on OEIS as term 8 of A390200

## Story

Counting shapes on a flat grid is hard enough, and on the hyperbolic plane the count had stalled at seven cells. I wrote a second program on different maths just to make sure the first one was not lying, and OEIS approved the new term.
