# Proof by exhaustion

> A proof in Lean 4 that 1 + 1 = 2. It is 616,873 lines long.

October 2026. Section: Maths.

Paper: [Proof by exhaustion](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23805053)

## Results

- Every number system built by hand, from Peano numerals to octonions and Roman numerals, with 1 + 1 = 2 proved in each
- No Mathlib and no shortcut tactics, checked line by line by Lean's kernel

## Story

Axiom Math's formalisation of the classification of finite simple groups runs to about 43 million lines, and it made me wonder how long a proof of 1 + 1 = 2 could honestly get. SAIR's Open Math OS gave me access to frontier open models, so I put 100 agents to the test. Lean's kernel checked every line they wrote.
