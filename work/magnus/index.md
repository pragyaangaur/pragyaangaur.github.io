# Magnus turning invariants

> Throw two balls spinning opposite ways at each other. Without air they can meet. With air resistance they never do, at any speed or angle.

September 2026. Section: Maths.

Paper: [Exact turning invariants for projectile motion under quadratic drag and a vertical-axis Magnus force](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30305)

## Results

- An exact solution for a spinning ball under quadratic drag and a vertical-axis Magnus force
- On arXiv as 2609.30305

## Story

The question was whether two balls thrown in opposite directions, spinning opposite ways, can curve around and hit each other head on. With air resistance the answer turned out to be never, and the paper went to arXiv on 28 September 2026.
