# NIT Agartala in Minecraft

> My whole campus at full size in Minecraft, one block to one metre, from the main gate to the hostels. You can walk through all three floors of the Library.

November 2026. Section: Things that run where they shouldn't.

## Results

- Roads, hills and ponds from real map data, and 30 named buildings designed by hand from campus tour videos
- One download, and it opens in Minecraft Java 26.3 or newer

## Story

The layout comes from OpenStreetMap and elevation data. The first pass looked right from above but the buildings were hard to tell apart, so the main ones were rebuilt from photographs until a student could recognise them on sight.
