# Forkestra

> Paste a GitHub profile and its year plays as an orchestra. Linus Torvalds' year makes a decent song.

August 2026. Section: Random things.

## Story

The last 53 weeks of a contribution graph become about ninety seconds of music. There are no audio files at all, so every instrument in the orchestra is synthesised in the browser.
