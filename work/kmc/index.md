# Keeping a copper catalyst alive

> Sulphur slowly poisons copper. The maths says copper only works in patches of three or more connected atoms, whatever the rate constants are.

October 2026. Section: The reactor.

Paper: [Rate-independent limits on a sulphating, self-renewing oxide catalyst](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15009719/v1)

## Results

- Preprint on ChemRxiv

## Story

I wanted to know whether the copper catalyst inside SkyCatcher could survive its own sulphur. I only kept claims that hold for every positive choice of rate constants, which is a strict diet for a kinetics paper.
