Keeping a copper catalyst alive

Sulphur slowly poisons copper. The maths says copper only works in patches of three or more connected atoms, whatever the rate constants are.

  • Preprint on ChemRxiv

October 2026I wanted to know whether the copper catalyst inside SkyCatcher could survive its own sulphur. I only kept claims that hold for every positive choice of rate constants, which is a strict diet for a kinetics paper.

The maximum area of the convex hull of a polyhex

In 2008 Sascha Kurz asked how large the convex hull of n hexagons glued edge to edge can be. It is at most ⌈n² + 14n/3⌉ / 6.

  • Kurz's conjecture settled, with the bound machine-checked in Lean 4

arXiv ↗OEIS A399934 ↗Lean proof ↗Palomar registry ↗

September 2026The question came from Sascha Kurz in 2008 and sat waiting for an answer. I proved his conjecture, checked the upper bound in Lean 4 and searched every polyhex up to twelve cells by computer, and the preprint went up on 28 September 2026.

They only meet without air

Magnus turning invariants

Throw two balls spinning opposite ways at each other. Without air they can meet. With air resistance they never do, at any speed or angle.

  • An exact solution for a spinning ball under quadratic drag and a vertical-axis Magnus force
  • On arXiv as 2609.30305

September 2026The question was whether two balls thrown in opposite directions, spinning opposite ways, can curve around and hit each other head on. With air resistance the answer turned out to be never, and the paper went to arXiv on 28 September 2026.

How long is a game of Uno?

I couldn't find out, so I proved it. With N players holding M cards each, a round lasts (27/19)NM turns.

  • 58 million games simulated in C, and big hands quietly hoard M/23 Wild Draw Fours each
  • Chess grandmaster Karsten Müller read it and wrote to me about its Nash equilibrium

October 2026On 7 October 2026 I wondered whether anyone knew how long a game of Uno lasts, and the answer was mostly no. So I simulated 58 million games, and a day later the paper was on Zenodo.
