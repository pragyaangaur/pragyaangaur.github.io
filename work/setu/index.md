# SETU

> SETU reads the solar wind at a spacecraft 1.5 million km away and forecasts the next hour of magnetic shaking for the North East Indian grid.

September 2026. Section: The Sun.

## Results

- Runner-up, IET India Scholarship Award, East and North East final
- 114 live forecasts, 0 false alarms, from a 19,000-parameter network written from scratch in NumPy

## Story

Setu means bridge, and SETU carries a measurement made in space to a switching decision in a control room. The neural network is written straight on NumPy with no ML framework, and the live nowcast ran until 20 September 2026.
