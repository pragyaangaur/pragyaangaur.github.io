# Beamline

> A signed random number every ten minutes, mixed from an ANU quantum vacuum device, NOAA solar data and the kernel. It started out as a thermoelectric cloud chamber.

August 2026. Section: Random things.

## Results

- 596 rounds and a Claude Pro prize, which nobody claimed

## Story

I put a Claude Pro prize on predicting the next number, and nobody claimed it. The beacon stopped on 31 August 2026 at round 596, and the chain is frozen exactly where it ended.
