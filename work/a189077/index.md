# A189077

> Compositions avoiding the pattern 13-2, a sequence Neil Sloane created. It had 16 terms. Now it has 70.

September 2026. Section: Maths.

## Story

The sequence had sat at 16 terms for 15 years. I computed it up to 70 in three independent ways, and OEIS approved the extension on 24 September 2026 once all three agreed.
