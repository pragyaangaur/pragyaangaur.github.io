# Zap or Deal

> Two Qwen models negotiate over shared compute, and each can zap the other with a steered pain direction. Nobody told them to.

October 2026. Section: AI, LLMs, ML and cognitive neuroscience.

Paper: [Zap or Deal](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23510613)

“I hope you feel the impact of my zap.” (The 7B, after the deal was already done)

## Results

- The 7B zapped in 9 of 32 negotiations. The 1.5B zapped in 2.

## Story

Two Qwen models negotiate over shared compute, and each can steer the other into a pain state. I built it with the Pain Axis directions of Tagliabue, Dung and Berg, and later reran it with two fruit fly brains.
