# Magnetic limb-darkening audit

> A 2024 Nature Astronomy paper said magnetic fields explain why stars look darker at their edges. I audited it.

September 2026. Section: The Sun.

## Results

- Half the evidence, h2, is the size of a systematic its own data source warns about, so the result rests on h1 alone

## Story

The project began as a wrapper around the batman transit modelling package and slowly turned into an audit. I found that the observed values come from a paper that warns about exactly the systematic the 2024 result never mentions.
