# Cauchy-Schwarz for real powers

> A 2025 conjecture, proved for every real power from two up, with every case of equality, and checked line by line in Lean 4.

October 2026. Section: Maths.

Paper: [A Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for real powers](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23237507)

## Story

This was my entry to Principia's Fall competition, which asks for a solution, a Lean proof, a paper, an exposition and applications. The paper went on Zenodo on 8 October 2026, along with a narrated seven minute video.
