A thank-you, in code

Cryo-EM and the porosome, in miniature

Joachim Frank won the 2017 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for cryo-EM. After his lecture at IIT Delhi with Bhanu P. Jena, I built a small working version of each of their ideas.

  • A ribosome rebuilt from 2,400 projections, each of which looks like static on its own
  • A vesicle docking at a porosome and releasing its cargo

Both lectures were mostly outside my own field of computational physics, and I enjoyed them anyway. As a thank you I wrote to each speaker with a small animation of their own idea.
