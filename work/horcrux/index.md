# Horcrux

> Tom Riddle's diary as a browser tab. Write on the right page and a small model answers in ink on the left.

August 2026. Section: AI, LLMs, ML and cognitive neuroscience.

## Story

I built it in one day on 30 August 2026. The model runs inside your own browser, so nothing you write in the diary ever leaves your machine, which is more than Tom Riddle could promise.
