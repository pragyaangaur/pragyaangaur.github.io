# TriKing

> After the Tetris movie I kept seeing falling blocks, so I made Tetris with three-cell pieces that touch at corners. Later I ported it to QB64 by hand.

Section: Maths.

## Results

- 500+ players, including a two-time Tetris World Vice Champion

## Story

TriKing now also lives on my jailbroken Kindle, where you tap to rotate and flick down to drop. It is one of the few games that look good on e-ink.
